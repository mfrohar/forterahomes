Everything You Need to Know About Renting a Large Unit in Ottawa

Ottawa's rental market has tightened significantly over the past several years, and the squeeze is felt most acutely by anyone searching for a genuinely large unit. If you're looking for a spacious 2 or 3 bedroom apartment in Ottawa — one where you can actually breathe, host a dinner, or set up a proper home office — this guide is for you.

Why Large Units Are Hard to Find in Ottawa

The structural reasons for the scarcity of large rental units in Ottawa come down to economics. Developers over the past decade have been incentivized to build smaller units — studios and one-bedrooms fill faster, generate more revenue per square foot, and are easier to finance in a risk-averse lending environment. Purpose-built two- and three-bedroom rentals represent a smaller share of new supply than at any point in recent memory.

For renters, this creates a genuine problem. The existing large-unit stock in Ottawa is aging — often meaning older appliances, shared laundry, poorer insulation, and management that doesn't always respond quickly. New quality supply in this category is genuinely rare.

What to Look For in a Large Ottawa Rental

Layout Efficiency

Square footage on a listing is a starting point, not a conclusion. A 950 sq ft unit with a poorly planned layout can feel smaller than a well-designed 860 sq ft suite. When evaluating floor plans for large rental units in Beechwood or anywhere in Ottawa, look for:

  • Bedrooms that are genuinely separated from the living area
  • A kitchen with adequate counter space and real storage
  • A living area large enough for a full sofa arrangement
  • At least one bedroom that can accommodate a desk without crowding the bed

Building Quality

The difference between a well-built purpose-built rental and an older converted building isn't just aesthetic — it's practical. Better insulation means lower utility bills. Quality windows mean less outside noise. Proper ventilation means better air quality year-round. When you're committing to a multi-year tenancy in Ottawa, the building itself matters as much as the unit.

New purpose-built developments like the Beechwood Collection at 82 Genest St are designed from the ground up for renters, with modern finishes, in-suite laundry, and layouts that reflect how people actually live today.

In-Suite Laundry

This one sounds minor until you've spent two years hauling laundry to a shared basement. In-suite laundry is one of the clearest quality-of-life differentiators in the Ottawa rental market, and it's worth prioritizing — especially in a larger unit where you're likely doing more laundry.

Ceiling Height

Standard ceiling heights in Ottawa rental buildings are often around 8 feet. A unit with 9 or 10 foot ceilings feels dramatically more spacious and airy — something that matters more in a larger unit where you're spending significant time. Don't overlook this in your search.

Understanding Ottawa Lease Terms

Your Rights Under Ontario's Residential Tenancies Act

Ontario's tenancy law provides meaningful protections for renters. Landlords cannot raise rent more than the annual guideline without approval, and tenants cannot be evicted without proper notice and valid grounds. Understanding these rules before you sign protects you over the course of a long tenancy.

Key things to clarify before signing any lease for affordable units in Ottawa:

  • What is included in rent (utilities, parking, locker)?
  • What is the policy on lease renewals and rent increases?
  • Who handles maintenance and what is the typical response time?
  • What are the rules around guests, pets, and alterations?
