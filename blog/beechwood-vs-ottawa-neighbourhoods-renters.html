Beechwood vs. Other Ottawa Neighbourhoods: Which is Best for Renters?

Ottawa renters have more choices than the usual shortlist of popular neighbourhoods suggests. While Westboro, The Glebe, and Centretown dominate most "best places to rent" conversations, the picture looks different once you factor in unit size, rental value, and livability. Here's how Beechwood Ottawa rentals compare to the city's better-known areas.

Beechwood vs. Westboro

Westboro is arguably Ottawa's most celebrated neighbourhood for renters — tree-lined streets, a strong independent retail scene, and easy access to the Ottawa River. It's also become one of the city's most expensive rental markets. Asking rents for 2 and 3 bedroom apartments in Westboro have climbed sharply over the past few years, and quality purpose-built inventory is frequently snapped up before it's publicly listed.

Beechwood offers a comparable experience — walkable main street, character architecture, proximity to green space — at a price point that's consistently more accessible. The commute from Beechwood to downtown is similar to Westboro's, and renters who've made the switch often describe the lifestyle as essentially equivalent, with more money left at the end of the month.

Edge: Beechwood

Better value per square foot for large rental units in Beechwood, without sacrificing the neighbourhood character that makes Westboro appealing.

Beechwood vs. The Glebe

The Glebe attracts a loyal cohort of Ottawa renters who value its village feel, proximity to Lansdowne and the Canal, and well-established restaurant and café scene. It's also one of the most constrained rental markets in the city — housing stock turns over slowly, and large units are almost impossible to find. The few three-bedrooms that do come available are held tightly.

Beechwood matches The Glebe on neighbourhood character while offering something the Glebe simply can't: availability. New purpose-built developments like the Beechwood Collection are bringing affordable units in Ottawa that Glebe renters have been unable to find in their first-choice neighbourhood.

Edge: Beechwood

For renters who want what The Glebe offers but can't find it there, Beechwood is the practical answer.

Beechwood vs. Centretown

Centretown is Ottawa's most urban neighbourhood — dense, transit-rich, and full of activity. For renters who want to be in the middle of everything, it's hard to argue against it. But that urbanity comes with trade-offs: noise, limited green space, and units that skew small. Developers in Centretown have focused heavily on bachelor and one-bedroom units over the past decade, and genuinely large rentals are rare and expensive.

Beechwood is quieter and slightly less central, but it offers something Centretown rarely can: space. A two or three bedroom apartment in Ottawa's Beechwood neighbourhood puts families and professionals in an environment that feels residential rather than urban-commercial — which, for many renters, is exactly what they need after a few years in the downtown core.

Edge: Depends on Priorities

Centretown wins on pure urbanity. Beechwood wins on space, quiet, and value. For renters with families or those who've already spent years living downtown, Beechwood is the clear choice.

Beechwood vs. New Edinburgh and Rockcliffe Park

These adjacent neighbourhoods are often grouped with Beechwood and share many of its characteristics: established trees, heritage architecture, and a calm residential atmosphere. New Edinburgh and Rockcliffe Park are also among Ottawa's priciest addresses, with rental supply dominated by single-family homes and high-end condos.

Beechwood South — specifically along Genest St — sits at the intersection of all these areas while remaining more accessible. The Beechwood Collection is bringing purpose-built rental options to this pocket of the city that simply didn't exist before.

The Bottom Line

Every renter's calculus is different. But when comparing across the key factors that Ottawa renters consistently cite — value, space, neighbourhood character, and access — Beechwood holds up extremely well against the city's more celebrated rental addresses.
