WHAT SETS US APART

Our Expertise

◆

Premium Finishes

Every suite at Fortera is built with materials that hold their look and feel over time — quartz counters, engineered hardwood, and fixtures chosen for longevity, not just price point.

◆

Transit-Oriented Locations

We build where people want to live without a car. Walkability, cycling infrastructure, and OC Transpo access are core criteria when we select a site — not afterthoughts.

◆

Energy Efficiency

Our buildings are designed to minimize energy consumption through smart building envelopes, high-efficiency mechanical systems, and EV-ready infrastructure that serves residents now and in the future.

◆

Tenant-First Design

We design for the people who actually live in our buildings — with generous storage, thoughtful layouts, in-suite laundry, and private outdoor space in every unit.

◆

Spacious Suites

At 860–920 sq ft, our 2 and 3 bedroom suites are among the largest purpose-built rentals available in Ottawa — because we believe families deserve real space.

◆

Professional Management

Fortera manages its own properties. That means responsive service, consistent standards, and accountability from a team that is genuinely invested in the long-term quality of every building.

◆

Community Integration

We choose neighbourhoods with established character and culture — then build in a way that adds to that fabric rather than disrupting it.

◆

Long-Term Thinking

We're not flipping assets. Fortera builds to hold and operate for decades — which means we make decisions based on what's best for the building and its residents over the long term.
